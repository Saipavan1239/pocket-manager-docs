Delete Account / Data

Instructions for removing Pocket Manager data.

Delete local app data

Use the app's Delete My Data control to remove locally stored application/transaction data supported by that feature. Android's app-storage controls and uninstalling the app may also remove local app data.

Request account and cloud-data deletion

To request deletion of your Pocket Manager account and associated cloud data, email:

p90449891@gmail.com

Use the subject “Pocket Manager Account and Data Deletion Request” and include the email address associated with the account so that the request can be matched to the correct account.

What the request covers

Where applicable, we will process deletion of the account and associated data stored in our cloud services, subject to any data that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other compliance reasons. The Privacy Policy explains the categories of information handled by the app.

For Google Play publishing: if the app allows account creation, the app should also provide a readily discoverable in-app path to request account deletion, in addition to this external web resource.
