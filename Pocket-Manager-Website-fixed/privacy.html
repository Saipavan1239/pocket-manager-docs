Pocket Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Manager handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Information the app handles

Depending on the features you use, Pocket Manager may handle transaction and profile information such as:

  • transaction amount, date/time, payee name, note, category, source, source payment app, status, and bank name when available;
  • transaction identifiers or UTR information when extracted or supplied;
  • information used for payee suggestions, categorization, statistics, and summaries;
  • account/profile information such as display name, email address, and account identifier; and
  • images or screenshots that you explicitly share with the app for transaction extraction.

2. Local storage

The app stores transaction and application records in its local Room database. Temporary app storage/cache may also be used while processing shared images and other app operations.

Local storage should not be described as encrypted unless encryption is actually enabled in the release build. This policy therefore makes no encryption claim.

3. Screenshot and OCR processing

When you share a payment screenshot or receipt with Pocket Manager, the app uses Google ML Kit text recognition to process the image and extract transaction details. The reviewed OCR implementation performs text recognition in the app using the ML Kit client.

The current codebase also contains local diagnostic logging around OCR processing. Such logs can contain sensitive OCR text. The release build should be reviewed to ensure sensitive diagnostic data is not retained longer or exposed more broadly than intended.

4. Firebase and cloud synchronization

Pocket Manager integrates Firebase services for account functionality and cloud synchronization. In the reviewed codebase, synchronization logic writes supported user profile, user statistics, daily statistics, and session information to Firebase/Firestore when synchronization is performed.

Accordingly, this app should not be described as a completely on-device-only finance application. Core transaction records are stored locally, while supported account/application information may also be synchronized through Firebase.

5. How information is used

  • to create, store, organize, search, and display transactions;
  • to categorize transactions and generate spending summaries and statistics;
  • to provide account/profile functionality and synchronize supported information;
  • to provide transaction, reminder, and summary notifications; and
  • to diagnose and troubleshoot the application where diagnostic logging is enabled.

6. Sharing and third-party services

Pocket Manager uses third-party technologies including Google ML Kit, Firebase/Google services, Android libraries, and other open-source dependencies. These providers may process information needed to provide their respective services and may collect technical information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Pocket Manager does not sell personal or financial information.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the app. No device, storage system, or network transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Retention

Local records remain on the device until you delete them, clear application data, uninstall the app, or use a supported deletion control. Information synchronized to cloud services may remain there until it is deleted through the applicable account/data-deletion process, subject to legitimate retention requirements.

9. Your choices and deletion

You can use the app's supported local-data deletion controls to remove local records. For account or cloud-data deletion requests, see Delete Account / Data.

10. Children's privacy

Pocket Manager is not directed to children.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, SDKs, permissions, data flows, or other data practices change. The effective date will be updated for material changes.
